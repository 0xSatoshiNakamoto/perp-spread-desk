    Adding an exchange

    Open this file in a text editor and find TEMPLATE near the top of the script. Copy it, fill in the exchange's market-list URL and fields, and add it to the VENUES list. Every table, chart and alert picks it up. If the exchange names an asset differently, add the name to the ALIASES list. The exchange's API has to allow browser requests (CORS); Hyperliquid, Lighter and Variational all do.

    Sources. Hyperliquid wss://api.hyperliquid.xyz/ws (fastAssetCtxs about every second, allDexsAssetCtxs for funding) and api.hyperliquid.xyz/info; Lighter wss://mainnet.zklighter.elliot.ai/stream (market_stats, about every second) and its REST API; Variational omni-client-api…/metadata/v2/stats every 5 seconds (it has no live feed). Your browser connects to them directly.
    Read the numbers carefully. Hyperliquid bid/ask are its impact prices. Variational quotes are its $1k quote and can be cached by Variational for up to 10 minutes (the age is shown). Variational has no price-history API, so its divergence line is built from what this page records while open. Lighter funding is its live hourly estimate. Variational reports 0% funding on many stock perps.
    Variational quotes arrive 20–80 seconds old (measured), so on a moving market they show gaps that are not really there. Quotes older than the max quote age in the top bar are shown with their age and left out of gap numbers.
    Pre-IPO and new markets price off each venue's own order book, so a gap there is not guaranteed to close. Variational's Anthropic and OpenAI markets have traded 2–15% below Hyperliquid and Lighter; check that each venue's contract uses the same unit before trading the gap.
